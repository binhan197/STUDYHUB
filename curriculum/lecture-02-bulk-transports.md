## Study milestones

1. Distinguish phagocytosis, pinocytosis, clathrin-mediated endocytosis, caveolae and transcytosis.
2. Explain the full LDLR pathway: binding → AP2/clathrin → dynamin scission → uncoating → acidified endosome → LDLR recycling → LDL cargo degradation. Compare receptor fates (LDLR, EGFR, transferrin receptor, FcRn).
3. Explain opsonization (IgG Fc/Fcγ receptor; C3b/complement receptor), actin-dependent phagocytosis, phagosome maturation and oxidative killing.
4. Compare constitutive and regulated exocytosis. Describe ARF coat recruitment, Rab specificity, SNARE zippering, synaptotagmin Ca²⁺ triggering, NSF/SNAP disassembly and neurotoxin cleavage of SNARE proteins.

**Coverage caveat:** Free videos do not individually cover every mechanism at graduate depth. Caveolae and endosomal receptor sorting have supplementary readings/animations clearly marked; JoVE may require a subscription. The original lecture's classifications should be learned as presented but are not automatically treated as universally applicable to all cellular pathways.
